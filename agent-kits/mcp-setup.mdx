# MCP Server

Connect VH3 AI to Claude or ChatGPT and ask about your jobs, engineers, sites, customers, quotes and invoices in plain English. Claude and ChatGPT call VH3 AI tools through the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) (MCP), and every answer comes from your own company's data.

Use **hosted MCP OAuth**. Sign in on VH3's page with the email and password you use for VH3 Connect. The server URL is:

```text
https://mcp.vh3.ai/mcp
```

<Note>
If you still have the old Claude developer test connector, delete it before you add this one: **Settings → Developer → Delete VH3**.
</Note>

### Sign in with your VH3 account

A VH3 AI page opens in your browser. Enter the email and password you use for VH3 Connect. Your password goes to VH3 only.

If sign-in fails, check the message on the page:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| That email or password is not right | Check both and try again. Reset your password from the VH3 Connect login page if needed. |
| Too many attempts | Wait a few minutes, then try again. |
| This VH3 account is no longer active | Contact your VH3 administrator. |
| Your company has not finished setting up VH3 AI access | Ask your VH3 administrator to enable it. |
| VH3 sign-in is not available right now | Try again shortly. |

### Choose what Claude may do

After sign-in, the page asks what to allow.

- **Read access** is always granted. Claude can read jobs, engineers, sites, customers, quotes, invoices and reports for your company, and runs these tools without asking each time.
- **Allow actions** appears only when VH3 AI offers a tool that can act for you, such as **Ask Connie**, which can send email and SMS, create drafts and queue quotes. It is unticked by default. Tick it only if you want those actions. Each action asks you to confirm first.

Choose **Allow** to finish. See the [privacy policy](https://www.vh3.ai/legal-and-compliance/privacy-policy) and [terms](https://www.vh3.ai/legal-and-compliance/vh3-ai-product-terms-and-conditions) for how VH3 handles your data. You can disconnect VH3 AI in Claude's connector settings at any time. To change your choice, disconnect and connect again.

### Try it

Ask Claude:

- *"How many jobs did we complete last week, broken down by engineer?"*
- *"Which sites have had repeat visits this month?"*
- *"Who could take job 12345 this week?"*

Claude calls **Job metrics** for the first question and shows a chart in the chat. For the third, it calls **Who could take a job, and when** and lists engineers with times.

### What you'll see in Claude

In claude.ai and Claude Desktop, many answers open as a card you can use in the chat. Claude Code shows the same answers as text.

| Ask about | Card |
|---|---|
| Counts, rates and trends | A chart. Switch the measure or the grouping, and compare two periods |
| Who could take a job | A picker of engineers and start times. Each slot is an offer; a person books it on the schedule |
| One job | A job card with status, customer and site, engineer, planned against actual times, and the VH3 summary |
| One invoice or quote | The status, dates, line items, VAT and totals |
| A list of invoices or quotes | A sortable table with a status filter and totals for each currency |
| One customer or site | Details, job totals, recent jobs and the VH3 customer summary |

<Note>
**Claude Code** can use the same hosted server. Run `claude mcp add --transport http vh3-ai https://mcp.vh3.ai/mcp`, then sign in when Claude asks. On Team and Enterprise plans, an Owner may need to allow custom connectors first. Tell your VH3 contact if a tool returns a "not found" error, because a few tools switch on as each release rolls out.
</Note>

## Connect in ChatGPT

Leave the OAuth settings as ChatGPT fills them in. Tick the custom plugin risk acknowledgement when it appears.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add custom MCP server">
    Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) → **Plugins** (left menu) → **Add** (top right) → **Add custom MCP server**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the VH3 AI details">
    Fill in:

    - **Name:** VH3 AI
    - **Description:** Connects to the Job and field service operations
    - **Connection:** Server URL
    - **URL:** `https://mcp.vh3.ai/mcp`
    - **Authentication:** OAuth
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave the Advanced OAuth defaults">
    Open **Advanced OAuth settings** and confirm the hosted defaults below.

    - **Registration method:** Client Identifier Metadata Document (CIMD)
    - Leave the Callback URL, the CIMD client metadata URL, and the default scopes unchanged

    <Frame caption="Add custom MCP server: name VH3 AI, URL https://mcp.vh3.ai/mcp, Authentication OAuth, and Client Identifier Metadata Document (CIMD). Leave the callback URL, CIMD client metadata URL, and scopes unchanged.">
      <img
        src="/images/mcp/chatgpt-add-custom-mcp.png"
        alt="ChatGPT Add custom MCP server dialog with VH3 AI, server URL https://mcp.vh3.ai/mcp, Authentication OAuth, and CIMD client registration"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the plugin">
    Tick **I understand and want to continue**, then click **Create as a plugin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your VH3 account">
    Find **VH3 AI** in installed plugins → **Connect** → **Continue to VH3 AI**. Sign in with your VH3 Connect email and password, then **Allow**.

    <Frame caption="Connect VH3 AI, then choose Continue to VH3 AI and sign in with your VH3 Connect email and password.">
      <img
        src="/images/mcp/chatgpt-connect.png"
        alt="ChatGPT Connect VH3 AI dialog with a Continue to VH3 AI button"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Open a new chat and ask:

    ```text
    Using VH3 AI, show me the latest five jobs and their current status.
    ```

    If ChatGPT retrieves the jobs, the connection is working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available tools

Once connected, Claude and ChatGPT can use these tools. They need no `company_id` or `api_key`, because your sign-in identifies your company.

### Jobs and sites

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **List jobs** (`list_jobs`) | Lists jobs with filters for status, type, customer and engineer. Filter by the date a job was created, or by the day it is planned |
| **Get job** (`get_job`) | Returns one job with worksheets and site history. Shows a job card in the chat |
| **Job metrics** (`aggregate_jobs`) | Counts, rates, rankings and trends for a period, with comparison and grouping. Shows a chart in the chat |
| **Investigate an operational question** (`investigate`) | Finds the cause behind a pattern, using evidence from your jobs |
| **Search past job outcomes** (`search_outcomes`) | Finds similar faults and what happened last time |
| **Weather for a job** (`weather_for_job`) | Shows the weather at a job's site and time |
| **Run sentinel checks** (`run_sentinels`) | Runs your early-warning checks on demand |

### Customers, engineers and search

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search customers, engineers, jobs and sites** (`search_autocomplete`) | Finds records by name, postcode or reference |
| **List contacts** (`contacts_feed`) | Lists customers with free-text search |
| **Get contact** (`contact_detail`) | Returns one customer with history. Shows a customer card in the chat |
| **Engineer skills** (`resource_skills`) | Lists the skills held by one engineer |
| **List resource groups** (`list_resource_groups`) | Lists your engineer and vehicle groups |
| **Who could take a job, and when** (`scheduling_suggestions`) | Offers engineers and times for a job, ranked by VH3. Shows a picker in the chat. It suggests; a person books on the schedule |
| **Company and platform status** (`company_snapshot`) | Shows your company profile and platform health |

### Quotes and invoices

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **List BigChange quotes** (`list_quotes`) | Lists quotes with filters. Shows a sortable table in the chat |
| **Get BigChange quote** (`get_quote`) | Returns one quote with line items. Shows a quote card in the chat |
| **List Quotient quotes** (`list_quotient_quotes`) | Lists Quotient quotations, for companies that use Quotient |
| **Get Quotient quote** (`get_quotient_quote`) | Returns one Quotient quotation with line items |
| **Quote totals and conversion** (`quote_summary`) | Exact quote totals and conversion for a period |
| **List invoices** (`list_invoices`) | Lists invoices with filters. Shows a sortable table in the chat |
| **Get invoice** (`get_invoice`) | Returns one invoice with line items and payment status. Shows an invoice card in the chat |
| **Invoice totals** (`invoice_summary`) | Exact invoice totals and outstanding amounts for a period |

<Note>
Reports and **Ask Connie** are switching on in stages. **Generate operational report** (`reports_generate`) and **Ask Connie (can take actions)** (`connie_chat`) appear in the list when they are live for your company. Connie through this connector sends email, SMS and notifications. It does not place voice calls.
</Note>

<Warning>
`investigate`, the totals tools and `scheduling_suggestions` can take a while on large periods. If Claude or ChatGPT reports that a call took too long, narrow the period or the question and ask again.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting a connection

### "Add custom connector" is missing

Your plan or organisation settings may not allow custom connectors. On Team and Enterprise plans, ask an owner to add the connector in **Organization settings → Connectors**.

### Sign-in opens, but Claude shows an error afterwards

Disconnect VH3 AI in Claude's connector settings and connect again. The connector URL is exactly `https://mcp.vh3.ai/mcp`, with no trailing slash and no path after `mcp`.

### VH3 tools disappear after working for a while

Your VH3 account may have been archived, or your sign-in expired after a long period of no use. Connect again. If sign-in then fails with a message, see the table above.

### Connie is missing from the tool list

Disconnect and connect again, and tick **Allow actions** on the consent page. Connie also appears only once it is live for your company.

### Your organisation blocks the connection

Ask your IT team to allow outbound HTTPS to `mcp.vh3.ai`.

### ChatGPT stops before the VH3 sign-in

Check the server URL is exactly `https://mcp.vh3.ai/mcp`. Authentication stays **OAuth**. Registration method stays **Client Identifier Metadata Document (CIMD)**. Leave the callback URL, the CIMD client metadata URL, and the scopes unchanged, and tick **I understand and want to continue** before **Create as a plugin**. Then use **Connect → Continue to VH3 AI** and sign in with your VH3 Connect email and password.
