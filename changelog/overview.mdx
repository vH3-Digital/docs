## New features

**Quote Documents in Connect.** Sidebar **Quote Documents** (`/quoting/documents`) is the working commercial record: letter, lines, Price, versions, and activity. Sales performance stays the Quotient quote list. See [Quote Documents](/connect/quote-documents) and [Sales and quotes](/connect/sales).

**Ask Salma on a Quote Document.** Streamed chat, apply or discard proposals, sessions per document. Full-screen on a phone; split pane on desktop. See [Quote Documents](/connect/quote-documents) and [AI assistants](/connect/ai-assistants).

**Add catalog parts on a quote.** Search make, part number, or title and add a line with quantity. Parts also appear in header search. See [Quote Documents](/connect/quote-documents) and [Search](/connect/search).

**My Cases and Create case.** Cases opens on **My Cases**. **Create case** is on the list: details and Team, then optional people. Filters match Jobs (status, type, priority). See [Cases](/connect/cases).

## Updates

**Quote Price and NTE.** The document header shows Total and Net. The Price tab breaks out buckets, VAT, and unpriced lines. An NTE over/under banner appears; Approve is still available when the quote is over NTE. See [Quote Documents](/connect/quote-documents).

**Job and Contact Connie.** The same streamed Connie as the global drawer. Job sessions are listed by customer. Voice notes sit on the composer. See [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie) and [Jobs](/connect/jobs).

**Chat chrome.** **New chat**, **Sessions**, and **Close** sit in the header. **+** opens prompt commands. Thinking chrome and tool chips are quieter; answers stream live. See [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie).

**Sidebar rail.** Desktop nav starts collapsed; hover or pin to expand. Quote Documents and Teams are in the rail. See [App overview](/connect/app-overview).

## Fixes

**Quote cards show the job Ref**, the reference your team uses.

**Parts notes keep line breaks** in markdown preview.

**Empty Case tags** no longer break the list.

## New features

**Quote drafts from follow-on work.** After a job, VH3 can assemble a draft Quote Document from the documented follow-on: catalog materials, Rate Set labour, and a letter that follows the priced lines. Open **Quote Documents** in Connect to review the letter and lines. An engineer or estimator reviews and approves before anything goes to the customer. See [Quote Documents](/connect/quote-documents).

**Estimate from the Intelligence API.** Server-side automations can start that same quote path with `company_id` and `api_key` on the [VH3 AI Intelligence API](/api-reference/overview). Recipients are the list you pass. The commercial send stays the Quote Document approve path.

**Guides for operating-system builds and corrections.** New guides on connecting the stack around the FMS you already run, and on keeping the judgment your team applies to AI inside your account. See [Building an AI-native operating system](/guides/ai-native-operating-system) and [Every correction is teaching something](/guides/every-correction-is-teaching-something).

## Updates

**Catalog sold units.** Measured lengths and pack sizes on a quote line convert to how the catalog sells the part. A 1,200 mm batten prices as one 3.9 m length, not millimetres times the length price.

**Blanking plates stay plates.** When the follow-on asks for blanks, the quote does not attach a consumer unit. Named fittings on the documented next job still price as usual.

**Job Ask Connie session titles.** Job-scoped Connie sessions get a usable title after the first customer-summary turn, so they are easier to find in history. See [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie) and the [Connie API](/api-reference/connie).

## Fixes

**Steadier first read after a quiet period.** Ask Connie and quote requests recover more cleanly when the intelligence layer has been idle.

</Update>

## New features

**Account Summaries for parent contacts.** Connie can produce an Account Summary for a parent contact — one overview that rolls up child Customer Summaries, account-level job history, and light commercial context (notes, invoices, and quotes). Account managers and ops leads can ask how an account is performing across the whole tree, not only one site or child contact. See [Operational discovery](/guides/operational-discovery) and [Connie](/guides/connie).

**Cases and Teams with Connie.** Connie can find Cases and Teams, create and annotate Cases, and set or clear a Case’s Assigned Team. Writes use the [VH3 AI Intelligence API](/api-reference/overview) (`company_id` + tenant `api_key`). In Connect, Advanced Search helps you find jobs, create a Case, link evidence, assign a Team, add comments, and follow activity. Teams are first-class: manage members and roles, then assign Cases to the right ownership group. Agent comments show Connie’s avatar on the Case timeline. See [Cases](/api-reference/cases), [Teams](/api-reference/teams), [Teams and cases](/guides/users-and-teams), and [Platform tools](/guides/platform-tools).

**Assign a Case’s Assigned Team.** `POST /cases/{case_id}/assign-team` sets or clears the Team responsible for a Case. Reassignment replaces the current Team; `team_id: 0` clears it. Activity records `actor_type` / `actor_id`.

</Update>

## New features

**Punctuality insight.** Ask Connie which jobs started or finished late, and rank them by the largest time variance so the jobs that need attention surface first. `GET /jobs/feed` and `GET /jobs/feed/account` accept `finished_late`, `finished_early`, `started_late`, and `started_early`. The same filters work under `filters` on `POST /aggregate/jobs` for late counts. Sort the feed by `endDeltaMins` or `startDeltaMins` for worst-N lists. See the [Jobs API](/api-reference/jobs).

**Clearer Connie session history.** Chat history returns clearer session titles (and related list metadata) so conversations are easier to find again. See the [Connie API](/api-reference/connie).

**ServiceChannel portal ingest.** ServiceChannel new requests and accepted proposals can flow through portal ingestion alongside other FM portals. See [Email Ingest](/api-reference/ingest).

## Updates

**VH3 AI n8n node 0.10.12.** Filter by Started Late or Finished Late, sort by start or finish variance, and use the same filters on account feeds and aggregates. Existing workflows stay unchanged unless you add the new fields. Upgrade from n8n Community Nodes or npm — see the [n8n community node](/n8n-node) and [node reference](/agent-kits/n8n-node-reference).

**Agent kits for punctuality.** [AGENTS.md](/agent-kits/agents-md), [Cursor rules](/agent-kits/cursor-rules), and the n8n node reference route “late”, “overrun”, and “worst-N” questions to the new filters and sort keys.

**Richer related-job context in search.** Related-work recent jobs now include job id and status for tighter follow-through from search results. See [Search](/api-reference/search).

**Notifications open the right item.** In-app notifications now take you straight to the relevant job, case, or record when a deep link is available. See [Notifications](/connect/notifications).

## Fixes

**Report engineer counts.** Engineer counts in reports use the people who actually worked the jobs, not roster headcount alone. See [Reports](/api-reference/reports).

**Tabular PPM imports.** Tabular PPM imports match the intended customer more reliably when branch and location evidence is present.

</Update>

## New features

**Intelligence layer launch.** The VH3 AI intelligence layer is live: a maintained operational graph, meaning-based search, structured job enrichment, and continuous monitoring across your field service data. See [Intelligence layer](/intelligence-layer) and [Introduction](/introduction).

**Connie.** Ask plain-language questions against your live operational graph and get cited, synthesised answers. See the [Connie guide](/guides/connie) and [Connie API](/api-reference/connie).

**Sentinels, cases, and teams.** Sentinels watch the graph 24/7 for repeat visits, SLA drift, dormant customers, and emerging risk — and can open a [case](/api-reference/cases), draft context with Connie, and route it to the responsible [team](/api-reference/teams). Read the [Sentinels guide](/guides/sentinels) and [Users and teams](/guides/users-and-teams).

**Email triage.** Inbound emails are classified, linked to the right job or account, and queued for the right team. See [Triage API](/api-reference/triage) and [Email triage FAQ](/faq/email-triage).

**Reports and briefings.** Scheduled and on-demand operational reports and briefings draw from the live graph. See [Reports](/api-reference/reports), [Briefings](/api-reference/briefings), and the [Reports guide](/guides/reports).

**Weather context.** Weather data is now available as first-class operational context for jobs and sites. See [Weather API](/api-reference/weather).

**Agent Starter Kits.** Ready-to-use configurations for connecting AI tools to the intelligence layer, including [AGENTS.md](/agent-kits/agents-md), [Cursor rules](/agent-kits/cursor-rules), the [VH3 product MCP](/agent-kits/mcp-setup), [Claude Projects](/agent-kits/claude-projects), [Claude Skills](/agent-kits/claude-skills), and [n8n agents](/agent-kits/n8n-agents).

**Verified n8n community node.** The official `n8n-nodes-vh3ai` node is [verified on n8n](https://link.vh3.ai/n8n), works on Cloud and self-hosted, and is usable as a tool by n8n AI agents. See [n8n community node](/n8n-node) and the [node reference](/agent-kits/n8n-node-reference).

**BigChange native integration.** Direct BigChange API coverage through the intelligence layer and the n8n node: contacts, jobs, invoices, quotes, sales opportunities, purchase orders, notes, persons, resources, vehicles, stock, worksheets, and reference data. See the [BigChange API overview](/api-reference/bigchange/overview).

## Updates

**Unified API surface.** A single OpenAPI specification now covers jobs, contacts, search, sentinels, reports, briefings, weather, triage, ingest, Connie, cases, teams, users, and user auth. Start at the [API overview](/api-reference/overview).

**Authentication and user auth.** End-to-end docs for tenant API keys plus end-user authentication flows. See [Authentication](/authentication) and [User auth API](/api-reference/user-auth).

**Ingest pipeline.** Continuous ingest with entity resolution keeps the operational picture current as customer hierarchies, sites, and job types change. See [Ingest API](/api-reference/ingest) and [Keeping the graph current](/guides/keeping-the-graph-current).

**Technology partners program.** Partner program details for FMS vendors, agencies, and platform integrators. See [Technology partners](/partners/technology-partners).

**Pricing published.** Transparent pricing for the intelligence layer, Connie, sentinels, and ingest. See [Pricing](/pricing) and the [pricing FAQ](/faq/pricing).
