# Jobs

Jobs is where you review work from your field service system inside VH3 Connect.

**Path:** `/jobs`

## Prerequisites

- Signed-in user with company data available from BigChange (or your connected FMS path).

## Browse jobs

1. Open **Jobs** in the sidebar.
2. Use the tabs:
   - **Active**
   - **Planned**
   - **Overview**
3. Narrow the list with filters (job type groups such as Call out / Service / Other, categories, statuses, and resources).
4. Open a job to see the detail drawer (`/jobs/:jobId`). Title format: **Job -** followed by the job reference.

The **Stats** tab is present but **disabled** in the product today.

## Job detail

Drawer tabs:

- **Job Details** — Overview (including AI summary), Contact Details, Timekeeping, Description, Engineer Details, Custom Fields
- **Worksheets**
- **Contact Details**
- **Job History**

Side chat on the job:

- **Summary**
- **Ask Connie** (job-scoped)

## Tips

- For “have we seen this fault before?”, prefer [Search](/connect/search) or [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie), then open the related job.
