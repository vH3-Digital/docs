# What is VH3 Connect?

VH3 Connect is the managed web app for day-to-day field service work on VH3 AI. Operators use it to review jobs, search the operation, manage cases, talk to Connie, and work with documents and quotes. Admins use it to manage the company, team, integrations, and billing.

**Open the app:** [https://app.vh3.ai](https://app.vh3.ai)

API and automation traffic continue to use `https://api.vh3connect.io`. Older hostnames such as `aios.vh3.ai` may still resolve; prefer **app.vh3.ai** in bookmarks and docs.

## Who this documentation is for

- **Operators and coordinators** — daily work in jobs, cases, search, and Connie.
- **Managers and admins** — team access, company setup, integrations, and subscription.

Developers building on the platform should use the [API Reference](/api-reference/overview), [Agent Starter Kits](/agent-kits/overview), and [Generative UI](/guides/generative-ui) guides. Those surfaces are separate from VH3 Connect as a product.

## How Connect fits

Connect is one way to work with the same operating picture that powers the API, sentinels, reports, and automations. Habits for briefing Connie and running discovery live in [Working with your operation](/guides/working-with-your-operation) and [Operational discovery](/guides/operational-discovery). This suite shows **where to click** in the app.
