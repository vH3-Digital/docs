# What is VH3 Connect?

VH3 Connect is where operators work the intelligence layer day to day. You review jobs from your field system, search the operation, open Cases for follow-through, assign Teams, and ask Connie in plain English. Admins connect integrations, invite users, and manage billing from the same app.

**Open the app:** [https://app.vh3.ai](https://app.vh3.ai)

API and automation traffic use `https://api.vh3connect.io`. Prefer **app.vh3.ai** in bookmarks.

## Who this documentation is for

- **Operators and coordinators** — jobs, Quote Documents, Cases, Portal Jobs, Teams, search, and Connie.
- **Managers and admins** — invite users, company setup, integrations, and subscription.

Developers building on the platform should use the [API Reference](/api-reference/overview), [Agent Starter Kits](/agent-kits/overview), and [Generative UI](/guides/generative-ui). Those surfaces are separate from Connect as a product.

## How Connect fits

Connect is one window onto the same operating picture that powers the API, sentinels, reports, and automations. Habits for briefing Connie live in [Working with your operation](/guides/working-with-your-operation). This suite shows **where to click** in the app.
