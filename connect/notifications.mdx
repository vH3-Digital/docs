# Notifications

Notifications surface alerts and updates for your user in Connect.

**Path:** `/notifications`

## Prerequisites

- Signed-in user.

## Review notifications

1. Open **Notifications** in the sidebar.
2. Use the tabs: **Unread** (badge), **Read**, **Cleared**.
3. Open a notification to follow its link when available.
4. Per row: **Read** or **Clear**.
5. Bulk actions: **Read all**, **Clear all**.
6. Open filters when you need to narrow by **Agents**, **Users**, or **System** (and clear those filters when done).
