# Contacts

Contacts is the directory of customers and related contact records in Connect.

**Path:** `/contacts`

## Prerequisites

- Signed-in user with contact data for your company.

## Browse contacts

1. Open **Contacts** in the sidebar.
2. Search the directory, or sort by **Name** or **Created** (default sort is newest created first).
3. Switch grid/table view if you prefer a different layout.
4. Open a contact drawer at `/contacts/:contactId`.
5. Use **Connie** from the contact drawer when you want assistant help in contact context.
