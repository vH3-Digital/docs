# AI assistants in Connect

VH3 Connect embeds AI assistants in the app. You do not install them separately.

## Assistants you will meet

| Assistant | Where | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Connie** | Header **Ask Connie**; job side chat; contact drawer; Advanced search selection | Operational questions, briefs, cited answers |
| **Tia** | Knowledge base document detail (**Ask Tia**) | Document-grounded chat |
| **Salma** | Sales quote detail | Quote-grounded chat |

Other agent names may exist in the platform registry for future or specialised flows. This suite documents assistants that have a clear Connect UI surface today.

## When to use which

- **Company-wide or ambiguous operational questions** → [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie)
- **Questions about a PDF or uploaded doc** → open the document and use Tia ([Knowledge base](/connect/knowledge-base))
- **Questions about a quote** → open the quote and use Salma ([Sales and quotes](/connect/sales))

## Habits that improve answers

The same briefing habits apply in Connect as in the API: say what is on your mind, name the audience, invite pushback. Full playbook: [Working with your operation](/guides/working-with-your-operation) and [Connie](/guides/connie).
