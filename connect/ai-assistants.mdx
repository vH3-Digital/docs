# AI assistants in Connect

Assistants are built into Connect. You do not install them. Use the right one for the surface you are on, then put lasting work on a Case with an Assigned Team.

## Assistants you will meet

| Assistant | Where | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Connie** | Header **Ask Connie**; job side chat; contact drawer; Advanced search | Operational questions, briefs, cited answers; can work Cases and Teams |
| **Tia** | Knowledge base document (**Ask Tia**) | Document-grounded chat |
| **Salma** | Sales quote detail | Quote-grounded chat |

## When to use which

- **Company-wide or ambiguous operational questions** → [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie)
- **Questions about a PDF or uploaded doc** → open the document and use Tia ([Knowledge base](/connect/knowledge-base))
- **Questions about a quote** → open the quote and use Salma ([Sales and quotes](/connect/sales))
- **Something that needs an owner** → create or update a [Case](/connect/cases) and [assign a Team](/connect/teams) after the brief

## Habits that improve answers

Say what is on your mind, name the audience, invite pushback. Full playbook: [Working with your operation](/guides/working-with-your-operation) and [Connie](/guides/connie).
