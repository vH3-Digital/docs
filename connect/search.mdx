# Search

Connect provides fast header search and a full **Advanced search** page.

## Header search (overlay)

1. Click the search field in the header.
2. Type at least two characters. Results group by entity type.
3. Optionally scope with filters such as **Search for all**, **Jobs**, or **Contacts**.
4. Open a result, or select **Advanced search** to go to `/search`.

<Note>
Filters for **Invoices**, **Knowledge base**, **Sales**, and **Integrations** are not fully backed yet. Prefer **Jobs**, **Contacts**, or **Search for all** for live results.
</Note>

## Advanced search

**Path:** `/search`

1. Open **Advanced search** (title on the page).
2. Enter a query; use **Filters** for facets such as contact, engineer, job type, and date.
3. Multi-select results.
4. From the selection bar:
   - **Create case** — open a case from the selection
   - **Ask Connie** — take the selection into Connie

Share controls are available on the advanced search page when you need to share a view.
