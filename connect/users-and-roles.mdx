# Users and roles

Invite people into your company and assign **product roles** under Profile. This page is company access — who can sign in and what they can administer.

For **operational Teams** that own Cases (regions, portfolios, incident rooms), see [Teams](/connect/teams).

**Path:** `/profile`

## Who can manage users

| Role | Typical capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | View, invite, edit, and deactivate company users; resend and revoke invites |
| **Manager** | View company users; edit company details; view invoices |
| **Standard / Engineer / Developer** | No user-admin permissions |

Roles you can assign when inviting: **Admin**, **Manager**, **Developer**, **Engineer**, **Standard**.

Admins and managers also change [sentinel settings](/connect/sentinels#sentinel-settings) and receive sentinel emails.

## Invite and manage users

1. Open **Profile → Manage users** (only if your role allows).
2. Invite users and choose a product role.
3. Edit users or change their role when you have permission.
4. Ask new users to complete [sign up / invite](/connect/sign-up).

One search box filters invites and users by name or email.

## Open and expired invites

**Manage users** lists open and expired invites, with who sent each one and when.

- **Resend** sends a new link and retires the old one.
- **Revoke** cancels the invite.

An invite link that has expired, been cancelled or already been used says so when someone opens it, with the date and who to ask.

## Deactivate a user

Choose deactivate on a user in **Manage users**. A deactivated user is blocked from signing in. Ask VH3 support to reactivate someone.
