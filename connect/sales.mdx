# Sales and quotes

Sales performance lists quotes and opens quote detail for review.

**Path:** `/sales`

## Prerequisites

- Signed-in user with quote data available for your company.

## Browse quotes

1. Open **Sales performance** in the sidebar.
2. Stay on the **Quotes** tab (the **Report** tab is disabled today).
3. Open a quote at `/sales/:quoteId`.

Quote drawer tabs:

- **Quote details**
- **Selected items**
- **Information** (disabled in the current UI)

Use share controls on the quote when you need to share it.

## Work with Salma

On a quote, open the Salma agent control (**Ask questions** / chat) for quote-grounded help. See [AI assistants](/connect/ai-assistants).
