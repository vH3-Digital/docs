# Sales and quotes

Sales performance lists quotes from Quotient so commercial and ops can review the same record. Open a quote, check the lines, and share it when you need to pass it on.

**Path:** `/sales`

<Card title="VH3 Quote Documents are a different product" icon="file-invoice" href="/connect/quote-documents">
  Follow-on drafts — letter, lines, Price, versions, and Ask Salma — live under **Quote Documents** (`/quoting/documents`).
</Card>

## Browse quotes

1. Open **Sales performance** in the sidebar.
2. Stay on **Quotes** (the **Report** tab is disabled today).
3. Open a quote at `/sales/:quoteId`.

Use **Quote details** and **Selected items** for the substance of the quote. Share controls are available when you need to pass it on.

## When a quote becomes operational work

If a quote review surfaces account risk, incomplete scope, or a customer that already has open Cases, open [Cases](/connect/cases) / [Contacts](/connect/contacts) and keep ownership on a [Team](/connect/teams). For company-wide operational questions, use [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie). For VH3 follow-on drafts, work the [Quote Document](/connect/quote-documents) and ask Salma there.
