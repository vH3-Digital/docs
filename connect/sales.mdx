# Sales and quotes

Sales performance lists quotes so commercial and ops can review the same record. Open a quote, check the lines, and ask Salma when the detail is dense.

**Path:** `/sales`

## Browse quotes

1. Open **Sales performance** in the sidebar.
2. Stay on **Quotes** (the **Report** tab is disabled today).
3. Open a quote at `/sales/:quoteId`.

Use **Quote details** and **Selected items** for the substance of the quote. Share controls are available when you need to pass it on.

## Ask Salma

On a quote, open Salma (**Ask questions** / chat) for quote-grounded help — line items, scope, and what is included. See [AI assistants](/connect/ai-assistants).

## When a quote becomes operational work

If a quote review surfaces account risk, incomplete scope, or a customer that already has open Cases, open [Cases](/connect/cases) / [Contacts](/connect/contacts) and keep ownership on a [Team](/connect/teams). For company-wide questions that are not quote-bound, use [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie).
