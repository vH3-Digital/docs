# Integrations and CSV upload

Integrations connect the systems your operation already runs. Connect uses a managed portal for many connectors, plus a CSV upload path for file imports.

**Path:** `/integrations`

## Connect an integration

1. Open **Integrations** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Review **Connected integrations** and **Available integrations**.
3. Choose an available integration (`/integrations/connect/:integrationType`).
4. Complete the provider sign-in / consent screens in the portal modal.
5. Confirm it appears under Connected when you return.

You typically need admin permission to manage integrations.

## Upload a CSV

1. From Integrations, open **Upload file** → `/integrations/upload-csv`.
2. Drop or select a **CSV** file.
3. Choose the upload type, watch progress, and confirm success.
4. Review **Recent uploads** in the drawer if you need prior imports.
