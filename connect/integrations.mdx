# Integrations and CSV upload

Integrations connect external systems to your VH3 workspace. Connect uses a managed integrations portal for many connectors, plus a CSV upload path.

**Path:** `/integrations`

## Prerequisites

- Signed-in user with permission to manage integrations for your company (typically an admin).

## Connect an integration

1. Open **Integrations** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Review **Connected integrations** and **Available integrations**.
3. Choose an available integration to connect (`/integrations/connect/:integrationType`).
4. Complete the provider sign-in / consent screens in the portal modal.
5. Confirm the integration shows under Connected when you return.

## Upload a file (CSV)

1. From Integrations, open **Upload file** (connected header action) → `/integrations/upload-csv`.
2. Drop or select a **CSV** file.
3. Choose the upload type, watch progress/status, and confirm success.
4. Review **Recent uploads** in the drawer if you need prior imports.
