# Billing and subscription

Subscription and invoices are on the Profile page. Invoice visibility requires invoice view permission (typically **Admin** and **Manager**).

**Path:** `/profile`

## View subscription

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Review the **Subscription** section for plan status.

<Note>
Subscription detail on Profile can vary by tenant. Use invoices and your Customer Success Manager when you need commercial confirmation.
</Note>

## View invoices

1. On Profile, open the **Invoices** section (hidden if your role cannot view invoices).
2. Review invoice history as listed.
