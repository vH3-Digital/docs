# Quote Documents

Quote Documents are VH3’s working commercial record for follow-on work. Open **Quote Documents** in the sidebar, review the letter and lines, add catalog parts, check costs, margin and price against the not-to-exceed (NTE) figure, snapshot a version, then ask Salma to propose edits. Choosing **Approve** checks the quote first.

**Path:** `/quoting/documents`

<Card title="Sales performance lists Quotient quotes" icon="chart-line" href="/connect/sales">
  Open **Sales performance** (`/sales`) for quotes from Quotient.
</Card>

<Note>
**Approve** records an internal checkpoint. Sending the quote to the customer happens outside this screen. Record the customer's answer with **Accept** or **Decline**.
</Note>

## Browse Quote Documents

1. Open **Quote Documents** in the sidebar.
2. Stay on **Live**, or switch to **Archived**.
3. Search with **Search Quote Documents…**.
4. Open a card, or use the card menu for **Reopen**, **Duplicate**, or **Archive** when those actions are available.

Each card shows customer, site, engineer, job **Ref** (the job reference your team uses), type, status, run status, total, and whether the priced net is over or under NTE.

On a phone, the list uses a tighter layout. Open a document at `/quoting/documents/:documentId`.

## Work a document

| Tab | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Quote** | Read and edit the commercial letter |
| **Quote Items** | Edit, remove and reorder lines; add catalog parts and custom lines; see costs and margin |
| **Price** | Check buckets, VAT, unpriced lines, NTE, and cost, sell and margin |
| **Versions** | Snapshot the live document, or open a past version |
| **Comments** | Leave review notes for your team |
| **Activity** | Follow who changed what |

The header shows **Total** and **Net**, plus how many lines are still unpriced. An NTE banner appears when the priced net is over or under the agreed cap. You can still **Approve** when the quote is over NTE — the banner says so.

**Show job context** opens a strip with the customer, site, contact, order, engineer and job.

### Quote

**Commercial Narrative** is the letter, in sections such as **Recommended works**, **Parts notes** and **Assumptions & terms**. The source job is collapsed by default; expand it when you need the originating job.

Choose **Edit** to change the letter. If someone else saved first, refresh before you keep typing.

### Quote Items

**Quote Lines** lists description, quantity, unit, cost, sell, and flags such as POA, TBC, or indicative. Indicative labour still shows its sell and joins the draft total — review those hours before you approve.

Choose **Edit lines** to edit, remove and reorder lines, or add a custom line. **Add part** searches the parts catalog by make, part number, or title. This works on a phone too.

**Costs.** Every line has a unit cost, with where it came from: your catalogue, typed in by your team, or unknown. A blank cost means unknown, and 0 means free. Price a line by **Margin** % or by **Markup** %, and switch between the two as you work. A **Waste / disposal** line type covers disposal charges.

Costs and margin show for your team only. They never appear in the quote text, the preview or the customer email.

### Price

The Price tab breaks out labour, materials, waste and the other buckets, then net priced, VAT %, unpriced counts, total, and the NTE row. Next to the total your team sees cost, sell and margin, and how many of the priced lines that figure covers.

### Versions

Create a version to checkpoint the live document. Opening an older version is read-only. Salma still works on the live document.

### Comments

The **Comments** tab holds review notes on the quote, the same way comments work on a [Case](/connect/cases).

### Activity

The Activity tab is the timeline for this document: edits, Salma's applied changes, approval check reasons and Client PO changes.

## The approval check

Choosing **Approve** first checks the quote:

- that the priced, POA and TBC lines add up;
- that the wording agrees with the lines — for example, the wording says a POA item is included, or quotes a price no line matches;
- that nothing the wording promises is missing a line;
- that no line is still marked POA or TBC.

Each finding shows on a card with the sentence and the line it concerns. **Fix with Salma** puts a suggested fix in the chat box for you to send.

Your company chooses whether a finding is advice or holds the approval until someone gives a reason. The reason is recorded in Activity. The approval check is switched on per company.

## Lifecycle

Actions depend on the document’s status and your permissions. Typical ones:

- **Approve** — internal checkpoint, after the approval check (see above)
- **Accept** / **Decline** — record the customer's answer. Accepting asks for an optional **Client PO**
- **Reopen** / **Duplicate** / **Archive**

**Client PO.** The Client PO shows on the quote header, apart from the job's order number. Use **Add Client PO** to add or correct it later. Each change is recorded in Activity.

## Ask Salma

On a Quote Document, **Ask Salma** is scoped to that document — the letter, lines, costs and price in front of you.

- **Desktop** — right-hand split pane
- **Phone** — full-screen Ask Salma

In the chat header: **New chat**, **Sessions**, and **Close**. Sessions belong to this document.

Ask in plain language. Answers stream live, with compact tool chips while Salma works.

- **Line changes.** Ask Salma to add a part, change a quantity or adjust the labour. Her proposal shows each line change with the new totals, and one **Apply** saves the lines and the wording together. **Discard** drops it. **Undo** puts the last applied change back, as long as nobody has saved the quote since.
- **Supplier PDFs.** Attach a supplier quote or price list and ask Salma to turn it into quote lines. Available where Library search is switched on for your company.
- **Hands-free.** Start a call from Ask Salma, say the change, and hear what she proposes. Say "apply" when you are ready. The change is marked **Applied by voice** on her card and in Activity.

In the composer, `/` or **+** opens your [prompts](/connect/prompts). The mic on the composer records a voice note.
