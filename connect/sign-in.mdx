# Sign in, passkeys, and reset password

Sign in to VH3 Connect with a passkey, or with the email and password for your company account.

**URL:** [https://app.vh3.ai/signin](https://app.vh3.ai/signin)

## Prerequisites

- An active VH3 Connect user account for your company.
- If you were invited, complete [sign up / join](/connect/sign-up) first.

## Sign in

1. Open [https://app.vh3.ai/signin](https://app.vh3.ai/signin).
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. Optionally enable **Remember me**.
4. Select **Continue**.
5. If company setup is incomplete, Connect may send you to [company and BigChange setup](/connect/company-setup) before the dashboard.

New users can open **Sign up** from this screen to register.

## Sign in with a passkey

A passkey signs you in with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, your phone, or your password manager. There is nothing to type and nothing to remember.

1. Open [https://app.vh3.ai/signin](https://app.vh3.ai/signin).
2. Select **Sign in with a passkey**, or select the email field and pick your passkey from the list your browser shows.
3. Confirm with your face, fingerprint, PIN, or password manager.

You need to add a passkey first (see below). Your password keeps working alongside it.

## Add a passkey

1. Sign in, then open **Profile**.
2. In **Passkeys**, select **Add passkey**.
3. Follow your browser's prompt to save the passkey to this device or your password manager.

After a password sign-in on a device with Face ID, Touch ID, or Windows Hello, Connect may offer **Sign in faster next time**. Select it to add a passkey in one step, or **Not now** to hide the offer on that device.

Passkeys saved to iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, or a password manager sync to your other devices, and show a **Synced** tag in Profile.

## Rename or remove a passkey

In **Profile → Passkeys**, each passkey shows where it was saved, when it was added, and when it was last used.

- **Rename:** select the pencil, type a new name, and select **Save name**.
- **Remove:** select the bin, then **Remove**. The passkey stops working for VH3 AI straight away. Most browsers also stop offering it; you can delete it from your password manager too.

<Note>
Adding or removing a passkey needs a recent sign-in. If you signed in more than 10 minutes ago, Connect asks you to **Confirm it's you** with your password or a passkey first. VH3 AI emails you whenever a passkey is added or removed. If you didn't make the change, remove the passkey and change your password.
</Note>

## Reset your password

1. On the sign-in page, open **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter the email for your account and submit.
3. When you see **Check your email**, open the reset message.
4. Follow the link to `/reset-password` (a reset token is required).
5. Enter and confirm your new password, then return to sign in.
