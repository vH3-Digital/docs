# Ask Connie

Connie is the global assistant in VH3 Connect. She reads your company’s operating picture and returns cited, data-backed answers — and she can work with Cases and Teams when the answer needs an owner.

## Open Connie

1. Sign in to [app.vh3.ai](https://app.vh3.ai).
2. Select **Ask Connie** in the header.
3. Ask in plain language, or type `/` to pick a [prompt](/connect/prompts).
4. Review the answer, citations, and any structured UI she returns.
5. Use **New chat** in the header when the topic changes. **Sessions** lists earlier threads. **Close** dismisses the pane.

You can also open Connie from Advanced search after selecting results (**Ask Connie** on the selection bar).

## Composer and streaming

In any Connie, Tia or Salma chat:

- Type `/`, or select **+**, to open your [prompts](/connect/prompts). Your most recent prompts sit at the top.
- The **mic on the composer** records a voice note.
- The message box grows to about 12 lines for long messages, then scrolls.
- You can stop a reply while it is generating.
- Answers stream live. Thinking and tool chips stay compact.

Under every message you send, **Copy** copies it and **Save to prompts** keeps it as your own prompt.

## Talk to Connie, hands-free

Choose **Talk to Connie, hands-free** and ask your questions out loud. Connie answers in the chat as you go, and the call keeps listening between questions until you end it. The message box lights green while she listens. Speech is recognised as UK English.

The same works with Tia on a Library document and with Salma on a Quote Document.

## Citations you can check

When Connie answers from your Library, each cited statement carries a page marker, for example *[Vaillant ecoTEC Manual.pdf, p. 13]*. Hover the marker to preview the passage, or click it to open the document at that page. See [Knowledge base](/connect/knowledge-base).

Links to VH3 AI pages in her replies open in the app. Other links open in a new tab.

## See what Connie is working with

A reply's **Usage** row shows how much of Connie's working memory the conversation uses, as **Context N%**. Expand it to see the parts: her instructions, tools, the customer summary, each turn of the conversation, and the data she looked up. When the figure gets high, start a **New chat**.

## Boards beside the answer

The numbers behind Connie's answer open on a board beside the chat: counts, charts, a map of sites, job lists and worksheet photos. See [Generative UI](/connect/generative-ui).

## Sessions

Connie keeps chat sessions so you can return to a thread. Prefer **one investigation per session** — for example one Case, one customer risk, or one standup brief. Keep unrelated topics in separate threads.

## Other entry points

- **Job** detail — side chat **Ask Connie**, the same streamed Connie as the header drawer. Session history on a job is listed for that customer. ([Jobs](/connect/jobs))
- **Contact** drawer — Connie on the customer, same stream. ([Contacts](/connect/contacts))
- **Quote Document** — **Ask Salma** on that document. ([Quote Documents](/connect/quote-documents))
- **Advanced search** — **Ask Connie** on a selection ([Search](/connect/search))
- **Prompts** page — **Use prompt** opens Connie with the prompt ready. ([Prompts](/connect/prompts))

## Example prompts

- "We have had three repeat HVAC callouts at this customer this quarter — what is going on?"
- "Draft three bullets for the morning standup on open risks."
- "Show me which jobs you used for that answer."
- "Open or create a Case for this, assign the North Region Team, and summarise what we know so far."

After Connie briefs, keep the lasting trail on a [Case](/connect/cases) with an [Assigned Team](/connect/teams) — chat is for investigation, Cases are for ownership.
