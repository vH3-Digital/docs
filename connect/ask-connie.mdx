# Ask Connie

Connie is the global conversational assistant in VH3 Connect. She reads your company’s operational picture and returns cited, data-backed answers.

## Prerequisites

- Signed-in user.
- Company data available in VH3 AI.

## Open Connie

1. Sign in to [app.vh3.ai](https://app.vh3.ai).
2. Select **Ask Connie** in the header.
3. Type your question in plain language.
4. Review the answer and any citations or structured UI Connie returns.
5. Start a **new chat** or switch sessions from the Connie chat menu when the topic changes.

You can also open Connie from Advanced search after selecting results (**Ask Connie** on the selection bar).

## Sessions

Connie keeps chat sessions so you can return to a thread. Use the session list in the Connie drawer to open a previous conversation or start a new one. Prefer one topic per session.

## Other Connie entry points

- **Job** detail — side chat tab **Ask Connie** ([Jobs](/connect/jobs))
- **Contact** drawer — Connie agent control ([Contacts](/connect/contacts))
- **Advanced search** — **Ask Connie** on a selection ([Search](/connect/search))

## Example prompts

- "We have had three repeat HVAC callouts at this customer this quarter — what is going on?"
- "Draft three bullets for the morning standup on open risks."
- "Show me which jobs you used for that answer."
