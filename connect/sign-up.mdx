# Sign up, invitations, and email verification

There are two common entry paths: **you are invited to a company**, or **you register and create a company**.

## Path A — Invited user

Use this when someone in your company sent you an invite.

1. Open the invite link from your email (Connect routes `/invite/accept` into join).
2. On **Join** *company name*, complete the form. The invite shows the role you were invited as.
3. Verify your email if prompted (**Verify your email** at `/signup/verify`).
4. Sign in and land in the app for that company.

You do not need BigChange credentials for this path unless your company still requires setup and you have rights to finish it.

## Path B — Register and create a company

1. Open registration (**Create your account** at `/signup/register`).
2. Enter first name, last name, email, password, and any other required fields.
3. Complete email verification (**Verify your email** → **Email verified**).
4. Create the company under **Company details**, including **Your company role**.
5. If approval is required, wait on **Pending approval** / **Review in progress** (`/signup/company/pending`).
6. Complete [BigChange setup](/connect/company-setup) when the app requires it.
