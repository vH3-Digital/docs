# Dashboard

The dashboard is home after sign-in. Use **Stats** for the operating picture, then move into Jobs, Sentinels, Cases, or Connie for the work itself.

**Path:** `/`

## View Stats

1. Sign in at [app.vh3.ai](https://app.vh3.ai).
2. Open **Dashboard** (or go to `/`).
3. Stay on **Stats** and set the date range (default **30d**).
4. From here, open [Jobs](/connect/jobs) for live work, [Sentinels](/connect/sentinels) for alerts that need you, [Cases](/connect/cases) for owned follow-through, or [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie) for a plain-English brief.

**Your views** at the top of **Stats** opens boards such as **Jobs on the map**. See [Generative UI](/connect/generative-ui).

Averages for jobs running early or late leave out jobs more than 8 hours off plan, so one outlier does not skew the figure.

<Note>
To see the work VH3 AI is doing for your company right now, open [Activity](/connect/activity) in the sidebar. The dashboard's **Recent Activity** tab may still show sample items depending on your company.
</Note>
