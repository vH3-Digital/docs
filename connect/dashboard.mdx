# Dashboard

The dashboard is home after sign-in. Use **Stats** for the operating picture, then move into Jobs, Cases, or Connie for the work itself.

**Path:** `/`

## View Stats

1. Sign in at [app.vh3.ai](https://app.vh3.ai).
2. Open **Dashboard** (or go to `/`).
3. Stay on **Stats** and set the date range (default **30d**).
4. From here, open [Jobs](/connect/jobs) for live work, [Cases](/connect/cases) for owned follow-through, or [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie) for a plain-English brief.

<Note>
Prefer **Stats**, Jobs, Cases, Notifications, and Connie for live operational work. **Recent Activity** may still show sample items depending on your tenant.
</Note>
