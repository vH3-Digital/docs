# Dashboard

The dashboard is the home screen after sign-in.

**Path:** `/`

## Prerequisites

- Signed in with a company that has completed required setup.

## View the dashboard

1. Sign in at [app.vh3.ai](https://app.vh3.ai).
2. Open **Dashboard** in the sidebar (or go to `/`).
3. Use the tabs:
   - **Stats** — operational statistics for the selected date range (default **30d**).
   - **Recent Activity** — activity feed in the UI.
4. Change the date range when you need a different Stats window.

<Note>
Prefer **Stats**, Jobs, Cases, Notifications, and Connie for live operational work. **Recent Activity** may still show sample items depending on your tenant.
</Note>
