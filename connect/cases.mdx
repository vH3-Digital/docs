# Cases

Cases are structured follow-through for investigations, sentinel-driven work, and team-owned issues.

**Path:** `/cases`

## Prerequisites

- Signed-in user.
- Concept model: [Teams and cases](/guides/users-and-teams) and [Working with your operation](/guides/working-with-your-operation).

## Browse cases

1. Open **Cases** in the sidebar.
2. Filter with scope pills: **Active**, **All**, **Closed**.
3. Narrow further with status pills (for example Draft through Archived) and type filters when shown.
4. Search with **Search cases…**.
5. Switch grid/table view as needed.
6. Open a case at `/cases/:caseId`.

Empty states point you to create cases from [Advanced search](/connect/search).

## Work a case

Detail tabs:

- **Overview**
- **Linked items**
- **Comments**
- **Activity**
- **Participants**

Typical actions:

- Rename the case (owner)
- Update priority (Low–Critical) and status (with optional note)
- Add comments for the team or Connie
- Add, change role, or remove participants

## Create a case from search

On Advanced search, multi-select results and use **Create case**. See [Search](/connect/search).

## Related concepts

Sentinels and reports are intelligence-layer capabilities. Connect surfaces outcomes through cases, notifications, and assistants — there is no separate Sentinels screen in Connect today. Read [Sentinels](/guides/sentinels) and [Reports](/guides/reports).
