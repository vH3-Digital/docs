# Team and roles

Team management lives under **Profile**. Access is role-gated.

**Path:** `/profile`

## Who can access this

| Role | Typical team capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | View, invite/create, edit, and remove team users |
| **Manager** | View team users; company edit; view invoices |
| **Standard / Engineer / Developer** | No team-admin permissions |

Roles you can assign when inviting: **Admin**, **Manager**, **Developer**, **Engineer**, **Standard**.

## Manage the team

1. Open **Profile**.
2. In **About Company**, open **Manage members** (visible only if your role allows).
3. Invite users and choose a role.
4. Edit or remove members if you have permission.
5. Ask new users to complete [sign up / invite](/connect/sign-up).
