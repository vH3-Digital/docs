## Sentinels

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are sentinels?">
    Automated monitors on engineer performance, site and customer risk, SLA clusters, scheduling, data quality, and commercial opportunities. There are 20: 14 for risks and 6 for opportunities. They run against your full job history from day one — no warm-up period, no waiting for data to accumulate. Most check daily at your company's set time; SLA Breach Cluster checks every 4 hours. They alert when thresholds are crossed, not on every minor fluctuation, keeping the noise low and the signal meaningful.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I see sentinel alerts?">
    On the [Sentinels page](/connect/sentinels) in VH3 Connect, in sentinel emails, and through the [Sentinels API](/api-reference/sentinels). The Sentinels page is switched on per company; ask VH3 support if it is missing from your sidebar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will sentinels replace my ops manager?">
    Sentinels prioritise attention. They give the ops manager a daily or weekly list of the patterns that merit human review, so the team can spend less time scanning jobs and more time deciding what to do.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are false positives handled?">
    Thresholds are calibrated during onboarding to minimise noise. Admins and managers then tune each sentinel in [Sentinel settings](/connect/sentinels#sentinel-settings): when it alerts, whether it is on at all, and a company ignore list of sites, customers, job types and engineers to leave out, such as training roles or contractors.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What severity levels exist?">
    | Level | Meaning | Suggested action |
    |-------|---------|-----------------|
    | Critical | Significant risk | Same-day review |
    | Warning | Negative trend | Within 48 hours |
    | Info | Heads-up or opportunity | Review on the Sentinels page |

    Each sentinel's settings decide what counts as a warning and what counts as critical. The consistency of levels is what enables deterministic, programmatic actions downstream: automations route Critical alerts to Slack immediately, open a case, or notify an account manager, all based on severity alone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did I not get a sentinel email today?">
    Sentinel emails carry only what changed: new alerts, alerts that got worse, and reminders for alerts still open (daily for critical, weekly for the rest). Acknowledged alerts, and snoozed alerts before their date, stay quiet. When nothing is new, worse or due a reminder, no email is sent. Check also that you are on the email list and that the lowest emailed severity covers the alerts you expect, both in **Company settings** on the Sentinels page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I stop seeing an alert?">
    **Acknowledge** it to show it is in hand, or **Snooze** it for 7, 14 or 30 days. An alert resolves by itself when the sentinel stops finding the problem. If it gets worse, it reopens.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do alerts include reference IDs so I can act in BigChange?">
    Yes. Customer-oriented alerts include the customer name and reference so you can look up the account in BigChange immediately. On the Sentinels page, each alert lists the jobs behind it; select a job reference to open the job.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
