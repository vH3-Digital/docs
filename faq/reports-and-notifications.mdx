## Reports, notifications, and operations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What reports are available?">
    Eight report types follow the operating day and week: `start_of_day`, `midday`, `close_of_business`, `day_review`, `start_of_week`, `midweek`, `end_of_week`, and `account_monthly`. Engineer, site, and first-visit-fix content appears as sections inside those reports. Continuous alerts are sentinels, not a report type. See [Reports](/guides/reports).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are notifications delivered?">
    Notifications can appear in VH3 Connect, email, and deep links into specific surfaces such as a sentinel board, report, case, or job briefing. Automations can also route notifications to Slack, Teams, WhatsApp, or connected systems through n8n.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
