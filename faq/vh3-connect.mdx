## VH3 Connect and developer tools

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is VH3 Connect?">
    VH3 Connect is the managed application built on the VH3 AI intelligence layer. Operators use Connie, review jobs and cases, search the operation, manage documents and quotes, and configure integrations. Admins manage team, company, and billing.

    Open the app at [https://app.vh3.ai](https://app.vh3.ai). Full how-tos: [What is VH3 Connect?](/connect/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are the Connect how-to guides?">
    Use the **VH3 Connect** tab in these docs:

    - [Get started](/connect/overview)
    - [Day-to-day](/connect/dashboard)
    - [Ask Connie](/connect/ask-connie)
    - [Admin](/connect/integrations)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are the generative interface components?">
    VH3 AI ships a toolkit of typed interface components for developers building on the intelligence layer: investigation cards, job detail views, SLA gauges, maps, report sections, and more. These components map to structured JSON from Connie and other tools.

    This is a development toolkit for custom applications and partner integrations. It is separate from VH3 Connect as a product. See [Generative UI](/guides/generative-ui).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
