# Changelog

What's new in VH3 AI. Updates are grouped by week.

## Week of 12 June 2026

The VH3 AI public docs site is live at [docs.vh3.ai](https://docs.vh3.ai), and the platform surface it documents went live alongside it. Highlights below.

### New features

- **Intelligence layer API.** A single, tenant-scoped API over the operational graph, with endpoints for [semantic search](/api-reference/search), [jobs](/api-reference/jobs), [contacts](/api-reference/contacts), and an [operational dashboard](/api-reference/dashboard). See the [intelligence layer overview](/intelligence-layer).
- **Connie, conversational assistant.** Ask natural-language questions about your operation and get cited, data-backed answers. Sessions, tool-call outputs, and provenance are documented in the [Connie API reference](/api-reference/connie) and the [Connie guide](/guides/connie).
- **Sentinels.** Always-on monitors that surface operational risks and revenue opportunities (engineer performance, site deterioration, SLA breaches, repeat-visit spikes, and more). Configure thresholds per tenant. See the [Sentinels API](/api-reference/sentinels) and the [Sentinels guide](/guides/sentinels).
- **Pre-visit briefings.** Generate an engineer briefing and call opener for any scheduled or on-the-way job, grounded in customer and job history. See the [Briefings API](/api-reference/briefings).
- **Email triage.** Classify inbound email by category, urgency, and routing action against your tenant taxonomy, with portal short-circuit for known senders. See the [Triage API](/api-reference/triage).
- **Cases.** Lightweight case management for escalations, complaints, and follow-up work that spans multiple jobs. See the [Cases API](/api-reference/cases).
- **Teams.** Scope users to customers, sites, or other entities for filtered views and access control. See the [Teams API](/api-reference/teams).
- **Weather.** Forecast and historical conditions for any coordinate, job, or site, with an `outdoor_work_risk` summary. See the [Weather API](/api-reference/weather).
- **Reports and briefings on demand.** Generate structured operational reports through the [Reports API](/api-reference/reports).
- **n8n community node.** The official `n8n-nodes-vh3ai` node ships with a unified credential covering BigChange and the VH3 AI intelligence layer, and is `usableAsTool` for n8n AI agents. See [n8n community node](/n8n-node).
- **Native integrations.** One-click connections to Xero, QuickBooks, Stripe, Gmail, Outlook, Google Drive, Slack and more, at per-user or organisation scope. See [native integrations](/native-integrations).
- **Agent Starter Kits.** Drop-in setups for Cursor, Claude Projects, Claude Skills, n8n agents, and an MCP server, so your AI tooling can talk to VH3 AI out of the box. Start at the [agent kits overview](/agent-kits/overview).

### Updates

- **BigChange API coverage.** Full reference for the BigChange surface, including [jobs](/api-reference/bigchange/jobs), [contacts](/api-reference/bigchange/contacts), [invoices](/api-reference/bigchange/invoices), [quotes](/api-reference/bigchange/quotes), [sales opportunities](/api-reference/bigchange/sales-opportunities), [purchase orders](/api-reference/bigchange/purchase-orders), [stock](/api-reference/bigchange/stock), [worksheets](/api-reference/bigchange/worksheets), and more. Starts at the [BigChange overview](/api-reference/bigchange/overview).
- **Authentication.** Tenant-scoped API keys with documented rotation and scope behaviour. See [authentication](/authentication).
- **Pricing.** Public [pricing page](/pricing) now lists plans and usage-based components.

### Fixes

- No user-facing fixes this week — this is the launch release.
